360° MDL Navigator

The agent that runs the 360° MDL method with you: from a topic sentence to a verified Core Knowledge and a Knowledge Distillate, under the Two-Key Rule. You keep every decision; it does the work and keeps the record.

Download the skill (zip, 340 KB) Source on GitHub Zenodo record
Install and start

Ten minutes to your first Core Knowledge

1

Install the skill

In claude.ai (web or desktop): profile menu → Settings → Customize → Skills → Upload skill → choose 360mdl-navigator.zip as it is, without unzipping. It appears as 360mdl-navigator; keep it enabled. In Claude Code, unzip it into .claude/skills/.

2

Prepare your topic and, if you have them, your exports

One sentence for the topic and the tier (Lite for a short chapter, Standard for a thesis chapter or a journal review, Advanced for a flagship review). If you already ran searches, export them: Scopus → CSV with abstracts, Web of Science → tab-delimited, Zotero or EndNote → RIS or BibTeX. Starting from scratch is fine too.

3

Start the conversation

Open a new conversation (ideally inside a Project for your review), attach the exports, and write:

Use the 360mdl-navigator skill. Start a 360° MDL review on "<your topic>", Standard tier. I have a Scopus export attached (stream SCO, slice IMP). Help me discover the other streams.

From scratch: Use the 360mdl-navigator skill. Start a 360° MDL review on "<your topic>", Standard tier, from scratch.

4

Turn the four keys

The Navigator stops and waits for you four times: Key 1 the Review Canvas · Key 2 the 6R scores and the core corpus (PASS and RESERVE items one by one, clear excludes in a batch after a 10 % sample) · Key 3 the key findings · Key 4 the Knowledge Distillate, dimension by dimension. Answer in plain words: "confirm", "R6 should be 2 because…", "merge dimensions 2 and 5". Nothing is final until you say so.

5

Take your Core Knowledge, then iterate

Every build gives you a folder:

360MDL_Toolkit_v2026_<topic>_v1.xlsxcalculator.jsongaps_report.mdmethods_draft.mdai_use_statement.mdmdlrc_checklist.mdnavigator_run_checklist.mddistillate/ (Markdown + Word, APA or Harvard)audit_log.jsonlreview_state_v1.json

Open calculator.json or the toolkit in the Index Calculator for the figures. Read the gaps report with your supervisor, add exports or DOIs, and build again: each version shows the indices before and after.

What it will and will not do

The rules the Navigator cannot break

No record without an identifierEvery item comes from an API response, an uploaded export or an opened page, with a DOI, PubMed ID, OpenAlex ID or URL. It never writes a reference from memory.
Proposed until you confirmScores, selections, findings and the Distillate stay "proposed" until you turn the key; the confirmation and any change you make are logged with your name.
Honest gapsEvery build ends with a gaps report: sources it could not reach, indices below target, keys not yet turned, what to do next.
The toolkit is the contractIt fills the official 360° MDL toolkit (T1–T17) and uses the method's indices and checklist; it does not invent formats of its own.

Closed databases (Google Scholar, Scopus, Web of Science) are not queried by the agent: it gives you the exact strings and import instructions. Open APIs (OpenAlex, Crossref, PubMed, arXiv) are used where your environment allows; otherwise the Navigator says so and uses web search with opened pages.

Worked example

It reproduces a published review exactly

The skill ships with the author's review Energy transition for smart cities in the Arab world (94 records, 56 core items, 15 key findings). Replayed through the Navigator, the indices come out identical to the published ones: SBI 0.92 · CovI 1.00 · MDI 0.86 · RI 0.90 · LOSO 0.80 · Convergence 5 %. The example also includes the Knowledge Distillate drafted by the Navigator for that review.
